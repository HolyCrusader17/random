RoN x ULTRAKILL - an ULTRAKILL style meter for Ready or Not raids

Needs: Ready or Not, ULTRAKILL (its music and sounds are read from your own install), UE4SS (Melty installs it).

In a raid:
  F7   cycle mode: CLEAN -> ULTRAKILL RULES -> POWER (shown above the meter)
  F8   show / hide the meter
  F10  write a diagnostic report to UE4SS.log

CLEAN            arrests, non-lethal takedowns and fast clears earn the most style; an unauthorized kill drops you to D
ULTRAKILL RULES  kills, headshots and multi-kills feed the meter; your score is the best rank you reach
POWER            everything earns style; higher ranks make you move and reload faster; at ULTRAKILL rank the next
                 hit you take is cancelled (and the rank drops)

The ULTRAKILL soundtrack plays underneath and shifts from a track's clean version to its battle version as your
rank rises. The first start indexes your ULTRAKILL sound files (can take a minute); later starts are instant.
Files: %LOCALAPPDATA%\RoNUltrakill (helper.log, clip-index.txt, clip-choices.txt, cache\)
